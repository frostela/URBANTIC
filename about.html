My Story

Real estate done the honest way

I started in real estate because I saw how stressful and opaque property decisions could be for ordinary buyers and renters. People were being shown properties that didn't match their needs, given inflated prices, and left to navigate complex paperwork alone.

Over the past 8 years across New Town, Salt Lake, South Kolkata, and the EM Bypass corridor, I've built a practice rooted in one principle — put the client first, every time. That means showing you only verified properties, being upfront about pricing, and staying with you from the first site visit to the final handover.

I'm RERA registered, work with a vetted network of legal and financial professionals, and personally accompany every client on site visits. No shortcuts. No hidden agendas.
